Schlage Smart Lock & RemoteLock App

Your front door uses a Schlage Connect smart lock. Use your PIN code on the keypad or the free RemoteLock Resident app on your phone.

RemoteLock Resident App — Free

  1. Download RemoteLock (App Store or Google Play)
  2. Create a free account with your email address
  3. Follow in-app instructions to link your account to your unit
  4. You can now unlock your door, change your PIN, and manage guest codes

Guest Access

  • 🔑

    Change your PIN: Set a memorable personal PIN in the RemoteLock app

  • 👥

    Guest codes: Create temporary codes for guests, family, or service providers with expiration dates

  • 🗑️

    Revoke access: Delete any guest code instantly through the app

If You're Locked Out

  • 1️⃣

    Try your PIN code on the keypad

  • 2️⃣

    Check for a low battery indicator on the lock screen

  • 3️⃣

    Contact your Property Manager: Midlands Property Management — 803-888-7060

🔋

Smart lock battery is maintained by the owner and replaced during annual inspections. If you see a low battery warning, notify your Property Manager as soon as possible.

🌡️

Ecobee Smart Thermostat

Getting Started

  1. Download the Ecobee app (App Store or Google Play)
  2. Create a free account using your own email address
  3. Select "Add a Thermostat" and follow the in-app instructions
  4. The thermostat is already connected to Wi-Fi — no network setup needed
  5. You now have full temperature control from your phone, anywhere

✅ You Control

  • Day-to-day temperature settings
  • Heating & cooling schedules
  • Fan settings
  • Vacation mode

🔒 Owner-Protected

  • Minimum heat (55°F)
  • Maximum cool (85°F)
  • HVAC alert settings
ℹ️

Temperature limits are set by the owner to protect the HVAC system and prevent frozen pipes. They are set at levels that won't affect your normal comfort. If you have a concern, contact your Property Manager.

SmartSensors

Two small Ecobee SmartSensors are placed in the living room and master bedroom. They help maintain accurate temperatures throughout the home automatically. If you see a low battery indicator, notify your Property Manager.

🔔

Ecobee Smart Doorbell Camera

You are the owner of this device during your tenancy. Set it up using the same Ecobee app as the thermostat — one app handles both.

Getting Started

  1. Open the Ecobee app and log into your account
  2. Select "Add a Device" and follow the instructions to register the doorbell
  3. The doorbell is already connected to the property Wi-Fi — no network setup needed
  4. Activate your free one-year Smart Security subscription: go to Account → Subscription and enter your redemption code: [REDEMPTION CODE]

Smart Security Features

  • 📹

    Live video feed of your front door from anywhere

  • 🗣️

    Two-way audio — talk to visitors remotely

  • 🔔

    Motion and doorbell notifications

  • ⭐

    With subscription: Video clips, package detection, and 30-day video history

After Your Free Year

You can continue with a paid subscription to keep recording features, or use the doorbell for free with basic live view and two-way talk.

📋

Move-Out: Please remove the doorbell from your Ecobee account — go to Ecobee app → Devices → select doorbell → Remove Device. Your Property Manager will handle the physical reset as part of move-out regardless.

➕

Adding Your Own Smart Devices

You're welcome to add smart bulbs, plugs, Amazon Echo, Google Home, and similar devices.

  • 📶

    Connect all smart home devices to DoolittleIsland

  • 🚫

    Do not connect smart devices to LunchIsland — this can cause interference

  • 🚫

    Do not install additional Wi-Fi routers or network switches

  • 💡

    When a device asks for 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz, choose 2.4 GHz and use DoolittleIsland
