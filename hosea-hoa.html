Pleasant Springs HOA

Community Rules & Guidelines

421 Hosea Court is part of the Pleasant Springs Homeowners Association. As a tenant, you are responsible for following all HOA rules. Any fines from violations are your responsibility.

HOA Management

Pleasant Springs Homeowners Association

Managed by PM Solutions SC · 714 S. Lake Dr. Suite 120, Lexington SC 29072

📞 803-996-4846 ✉️ Email HOA
⚠️

Important: Any HOA fines resulting from your violation of community rules are your sole financial responsibility per your lease. HOA dues are paid by the owner. Please read all rules carefully.

🚗

Parking & Vehicles

  • ✅

    Park only in your garage or driveway. No parking on grass.

  • ⏱️

    Overflow parking is temporary — max 48 hours. Repeat violations: $50/day fine.

  • 🌙

    No overnight street parking for residents. Overnight guests must use overflow parking. Street parking only permitted when overflow is full, daytime only.

  • 🚛

    Commercial vehicles, RVs, trailers, boats, and watercraft must be kept inside the garage. Loading/unloading exception: 24 hours max, not more than once per 10-day period. Fine: $50/day plus possible towing.

  • 🔧

    No vehicle repairs outside unless an emergency completable within 4 hours. Vehicle must then be removed.

🌿

Lawn & Landscaping

  • ✅

    The HOA provides front lawn cutting, edging, and blowing. No action needed on your part.

  • 🌱

    All hedges, plants, flowers, and mulch must be kept neat and attractive at all times.

  • 🚫

    No above-ground pools or unapproved irrigation systems.

  • 🚫

    No tents, trailers, or temporary structures placed on the property.

🐾

Pets & Animals

  • 🐶

    Common household pets only — dogs and cats. No livestock. Maximum 2 pets per household.

  • 🦮

    Leash required at all times outside. Dogs may only be walked in HOA-designated areas.

  • 🧹

    Clean up after your pet immediately. Fine: $25 per occurrence.

  • 🔇

    Pets must not create excessive noise or nuisance to neighbors.

🔇

Noise & Nuisance

  • 🔇

    No loud radios, parties, or activities causing discomfort or annoyance to neighbors.

  • 🧹

    Keep property in a clean, tidy, and attractive condition at all times.

  • 🗑️

    Garbage cans must be screened from view. Do not leave bins at the curb between pickup days.

  • 🚫

    No vehicle assembly/disassembly or mechanical work that creates unsightly conditions.

📡

Signs, Antennas & Exterior

  • 📢

    No signs except one For Sale/For Rent sign max 24" × 18". Small security signs permitted in the front flower bed only.

  • 📡

    No exterior TV or radio antennas. Small satellite dishes permitted on side or back of home — must be maintained and removed when not in use.

  • 🎨

    Any exterior changes (paint, fencing, additions, driveway) require written ARB approval before starting.

  • 🔫

    No firearms of any type — including BB guns, pellet guns, and all others regardless of size.

⚠️

Violations & Fines

  • 💰

    Overflow parking violations: $50/day

  • 💰

    Commercial vehicle violations: $50/day + possible towing at owner's expense

  • 💰

    Pet waste violations: $25 per occurrence

  • 💰

    ARB / exterior violations: $50/day until corrected

📄

If you receive an HOA violation notice, contact your Property Manager immediately. Do not ignore HOA correspondence. All fines from tenant conduct are the tenant's financial responsibility per your lease.
