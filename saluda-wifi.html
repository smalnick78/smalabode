Wi-Fi Tips & Troubleshooting

  • 📶

    Slow connection? Move closer to one of the white Wi-Fi access points mounted on the walls or ceiling — there is one on each floor.

  • 🔄

    Lost connection? Wait 5–10 minutes — the system has automatic recovery built in. If it doesn't come back, contact your Property Manager.

  • 🚫

    Do not unplug or reset any networking equipment. If you suspect a hardware issue, contact your Property Manager.

  • 🔒

    Other networks visible nearby are not for tenant use. Connect only to LunchIsland and DoolittleIsland.

🔔

Internet Outage Notifications
