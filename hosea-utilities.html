Services & Utilities

Utilities & Services

Contact information and setup details for all utilities at 421 Hosea Court. Set up accounts in your name on or before your lease start date.

📋

Tenant responsibility: Electric, gas, water/sewer, and trash are in the tenant's name and paid by the tenant. Internet service is included with the rental — no setup required.

Internet

Kinetic by Windstream

High-speed fiber internet is included with your rental and already active. No setup required on your end.

For outages, contact your Property Manager. To receive outage notifications by text or call, let PM know at move-in.

🌐 kinetic.com →

Electric & Gas

Dominion Energy South Carolina

Set up your electric and gas account as soon as possible to ensure uninterrupted service.

📞 1-800-251-7234 — 24/7 emergency line

🌐 dominionenergy.com →

Water & Sewer

Joint Municipal Water & Sewer

Set up your water and sewer account with Lexington County's Joint Municipal Water & Sewer Commission.

Contact LCJMWSC to establish service in your name before move-in.

🌐 lcjmwsc.com →

Trash & Recycling

Waste Management

Trash and recycling pickup is provided through Waste Management.

🗓️ Pickup Day: Fridays. Place bins at curb by 7am. Per HOA rules, bins must be out of view between pickup days.

🌐 wm.com →
🚨

Utility Emergencies

  • 🔥

    Gas leak or electrical emergency: Leave the property immediately and call Dominion Energy at 1-800-251-7234 (24/7), then call 911 if needed. Notify your Property Manager as well.

  • 💧

    Water emergency / burst pipe: Locate and shut off the main water valve, then contact your Property Manager immediately at 803-888-7060.

  • 📡

    Internet outage: Wait 5–10 minutes for automatic recovery. If the outage persists, contact your Property Manager.

421 Hosea Court, Lexington SC · Midlands Property Management · 803-888-7060
